What makes Wolaita the best East Africa vacation spots?

From the bustling city centers to the serene mountain vistas, there are so many places to visit in Wolaita that you'll want to return to time and time again. Wolaita offers an urban edge that inspires the artist in all of us.

Boutiques and unique events dot the walk along the cultural trails. You can also tour museums, catch a show or explore art installations. In Lake Abaya and nearby springs, you've got beaches, natural resorts and entertainment to the max.
